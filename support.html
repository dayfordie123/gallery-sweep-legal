Common questions

Does Gallery Sweep upload my library?

No. Version 1 media analysis runs on the device. Apple, Google, or the operating system may download your own cloud-backed item when you request it.

Why are results incomplete?

Selected Photos Only mode covers only allowed items. Cloud-only originals, unavailable sizes, unsupported formats, or conservative thresholds can also limit results.

How do I remove local scan data?

Open Settings → Clear Local Scan Index. This removes derived app data, not photos or videos.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open Settings → Manage Subscription and cancel through Apple or Google. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore Pro?

Use Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in to the store account that purchased the subscription.

Was my photo permanently deleted?

The app requests the system to move approved items to Recently Deleted or Trash. Recovery and retention depend on your device and cloud settings.
